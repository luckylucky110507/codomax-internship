# Day 6 – Data Visualization

## Task
Create scatter plots, bar charts, and line charts using Matplotlib to visualize patterns and relationships in the dataset.

## Concepts Covered
- Importing and using Matplotlib (`matplotlib.pyplot`)
- Creating a scatter plot to visualize the relationship between study hours and math score
- Creating a bar chart to visualize gender distribution using `value_counts().plot()`
- Creating a line chart to visualize the trend between study hours and math score
- Adding labels and titles to charts for clarity

## Files in this Folder
- `Data_visualization.ipynb` – Notebook containing all three visualizations
- `README.md` – Documentation for this day's task
- `screenshots/` – Proof of code and chart output
  - `jupyter_scatter_plot.png` – Scatter plot of study hours vs. math score
  - `jupyter_bar_chart.png` – Bar chart of gender distribution
  - `jupyter_line_chart.png` – Line chart showing study hours vs. math score trend

## Key Observations
- Students with more weekly study hours generally tend to have higher math scores
- The dataset has a fairly even distribution between male and female students
- The line chart highlights fluctuations in scores, with an overall upward trend as study hours increase

## Expected Outcome
Basic charts created to visualize relationships and distributions within the dataset.
